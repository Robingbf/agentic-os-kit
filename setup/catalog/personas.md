# Personas — starting points, never templates

Use these to give the user examples when they are unsure. Always design from **their** answers.

| Persona | Typical areas | Pages | Routines | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Developer / indie maker** | each app or client project, admin | Projects (tasks + session journals), Business (revenue vs costs) | morning digest (store reviews, errors, payments, mail), nightly map check | release notes from commits, bug triage from an error log, "where did I stop on X" |
| **Content creator / videographer** | channel(s), sponsors, production, admin | **Content** (pipeline: ideas → scripted → shot → edited → published; stats KPIs), Sponsors | weekly stats refresh → `page-content.json`, sponsor deadlines digest | script outline from notes, title/thumbnail ideas, repurpose a video into posts, sponsor email draft |
| **Freelancer / consultant** | each client, prospects, admin & invoicing | **Clients** (status, next step, last contact, unpaid invoices) | invoice & payment follow-up, client mail digest, weekly hours recap | client brief before a call, proposal draft from a template, meeting notes → tasks |
| **Founder / small business / e-commerce** | product, sales, ops, finance, team | Business, **Sales** (pipeline), Projects | daily KPIs (orders, revenue, refunds), supplier/customer mail digest | customer reply draft, weekly investor/partner update, competitor watch summary |
| **Student / researcher** | each course, thesis, admin, job search | **Studies** (exams, deadlines, readings), Projects | weekly deadline sweep (portal emails, calendar), reading list refresh | summarise a paper into notes, flashcards from a chapter, essay outline |
| **Employee / manager** | team, projects, 1:1s, admin | Projects, **People** (1:1 notes, follow-ups) | morning digest (meetings, mentions, waiting on you), Friday report draft | meeting prep from calendar + docs, status update draft, decision log entry |
| **Job seeker** | applications, networking, skills | **Applications** (company, stage, next action, date) | new offers digest from job alerts, follow-up reminders | tailor CV to an offer, cover letter draft, interview prep sheet |
| **Creative (photo, design, music)** | clients, portfolio, gear, admin | **Bookings** or **Portfolio**, Business | inquiries digest, delivery deadlines | client brief, shot list / moodboard notes, quote draft |
| **Personal life / family admin** | home, health, money, kids, travel | **Home** (bills, renewals, appointments) | bills & renewals sweep (mail), appointments in Next days | paperwork checklist for a procedure, trip plan, budget summary |

Panels that are often **off**: Inbox and Waiting on you for people who live in Slack rather than email;
Business for people with no revenue to track; quarter grid for people without dated goals.
