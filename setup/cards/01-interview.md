# Card 01 — Interview: who they are and what their weeks look like

**Goal:** understand the person well enough to design an OS that removes real friction. 15–25 minutes.

Ask **one question at a time**, wait, dig when vague, and append each Q/A immediately to
`interviews/<today>-<topic>.md` (topics: `work`, `week`, `obligations`, `forgetting`, `wishes`).

## Topics, in order

1. **What they do.** "In one or two sentences, what do you do, and for whom?" Then: main activity vs
   side activities; employee, freelancer, founder, creator, student, other; how they earn money (if
   relevant and they want to say). Identify their **areas**: the 3 to 7 big parts of their life/work
   (e.g. "Client work", "YouTube channel", "Studies", "Home & admin").
2. **Their week.** "What are the 5 things you do every week, without fail?" For each: how long it
   takes, what tool they use, what is annoying about it.
3. **Obligations.** "Who are you waiting on, and who is waiting on you right now?" Deadlines, recurring
   duties (taxes, invoices, reports, uploads, exams, rent…), people they must answer.
4. **What slips.** "What do you regularly forget, find out too late, or have to search for again?"
5. **Information scattered.** "Which things do you check in several places every day?" (mail, calendar,
   tasks, stats, messages, bank…). This is what the dashboard should gather.
6. **Mornings.** "If one page could tell you something every morning, what should it say?"
7. **Projects and goals.** "What are you working towards in the next 3 months? Any date that matters?"
   (launch, exam, delivery, trip…) → candidate milestones.
8. **Limits.** "Is there anything I must never read or touch?" (private folders, family, health, a
   former employer…) → `off_limits` (exact folders are confirmed in card 03).

Push back kindly on vague answers. If they ask "what do others do?", give 2–3 examples from
`catalog/personas.md`, never pressure.

## When they say "stop" or after topic 8

Write `interviews/<today>-summary.md`: who they are (5 lines), their areas (draft), recurring tasks,
obligations, pain points ranked **by them** (ask: "which one would you remove first if you could?"),
milestones, and **what you still do not know**. Show the summary, let them correct it.

## Done when
The summary is validated by the user. Next: card 02 (their tools).
